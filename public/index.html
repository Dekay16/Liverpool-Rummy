How to Play Liverpool Rummy

Welcome to Liverpool Rummy! The goal is to have the lowest score after 7 rounds by getting rid of all your cards.

Starting the Game

  • Enter a Game ID, your Name, and pick a starting round (defaults to 1), then click "Join Game". Share the Game ID with friends to play together.
  • Game starts with 2+ players. Each gets 10 cards, and one card is in the discard pile.

Gameplay

Each round has a goal (e.g., "2 Sets"). Check "Round" for the current goal.

  • Turn Actions:
    • First Step: Click "Pass Discard" (others can buy) or "Take Discard" (take it yourself).
    • Draw: After choosing, click "Draw from Deck" if you passed.
    • Buy Discard: When offered, click "Buy Discard" (costs 2 cards).
  • Going Down:
    • Select cards with checkboxes, click "Go Down". Sets (3+ same rank) and runs (4+ sequential, same suit) are auto-detected.
    • Must match the round’s exact goal (e.g., 2 sets).
  • Laying Off: (After Going Down)
    • Select cards, choose player and meld, click "Layoff".
  • Discard: Select one card, click "Discard Selected" to end turn.
  • Going Out: After going down, discard last card to end round.

Winning

  • First to go out ends the round. Others score hand cards (Jokers=20, Aces=15, Face=10, Others=5).
  • Lowest score after 7 rounds wins!
